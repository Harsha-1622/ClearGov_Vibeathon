**ClearGov **– From Evidence to Decision is an AI-powered public-service application that analyzes a selected service and identifies the documents required to apply. It compares the required documents with the evidence provided by the applicant, detects missing or problematic documents, explains why each document is needed, and guides the applicant on the next steps. ClearGov aims to make government application processes simpler, more transparent, and easier to understand for citizens.
Important Features of ClearGov :
**1. Multi-Language Support**
Allows applicants to use ClearGov in multiple languages, making document requirements, explanations, missing-evidence messages, and next-step guidance easier to understand.
**2. AI Requirement Analyzer**
Analyzes the selected public-service application and identifies the documents required for that specific application.
**3. Smart Document Checklist**
Generates a clear list of required, conditional, and currently unnecessary documents based on the applicant's information.
**4. Missing Document Detection**
Compares required documents with submitted evidence and automatically identifies exactly which documents are missing.
**5. Requirement Explanation**
Explains why each document is required and which application requirement it supports.
**6. Evidence Gap Detection**
Identifies gaps between what the application requires and what the applicant has provided.
**7. Document Upload & Tracking**
Allows applicants to upload supporting documents and dynamically updates their evidence status.
**8. Smart Next Action**
Tells the applicant what they should do next, such as "Upload Income Certificate".
**9. Conditional Document Requirements**
Identifies additional documents based on applicant-specific conditions, such as income-based benefits or disability assistance.
**10. Evidence Readiness**
Shows how many required documents have been provided and how many are still missing.
**11. Evidence Problem Detection**
Supports problematic evidence states such as missing, incomplete, unreadable, expired, conflicting, and low-confidence documents.
**12. Evidence → Requirement Mapping**
Shows how each submitted document supports a particular application requirement.
**13. New Applicant Mode**
Provides a completely blank application form so new users enter their own information without receiving demo data.
**14. Smart Form Validation**
Validates required fields, dates, income values, selections, and invalid inputs before allowing the applicant to continue.
**15. Demo Mode**
Provides predefined applicants such as Ananya, Rahul, and Priya to demonstrate different evidence scenarios.
**16. Dynamic Reassessment**
When a missing document is uploaded, the evidence count and application status update automatically.
**17. Conflict Detection**
Identifies contradictory information between submitted sources instead of automatically choosing one.
**18. Audit Trail**
Maintains a timeline of important application and evidence events for transparency.
